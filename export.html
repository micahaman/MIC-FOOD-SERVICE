Export & International Distribution

Miguelitos products are available in markets around the world — served through a network of trusted importers, distributors, and retail partners.

From our manufacturing facility in the Philippines, we supply food and beverage products to markets across Asia, the Middle East, and beyond. Our export portfolio covers the same product range available domestically — formulated, packed, and certified for international trade.
