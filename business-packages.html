Business Packages

Everything you need to start a food business — in one package.

Miguelitos Business Packages are designed for entrepreneurs, stall owners, and food businesses that want a proven concept without the guesswork. Each package combines commercial-grade equipment with Miguelitos' best-selling premixes, so you can open faster, operate consistently, and restock easily.

These are not franchise arrangements. You own your equipment, run your own business, and order supplies directly from us whenever you need to restock.
