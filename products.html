Our Products

Miguelitos manufactures and supplies a wide range of food and beverage products — from retail-ready packs to bulk foodservice formats. Our portfolio covers soft serve premixes, beverages, coffee, bakery mixes, savory solutions, and Filipino specialty products, alongside the machines and equipment to serve them.

Products are available in single-serve, retail, foodservice, and institutional/bulk formats. Pack size availability varies by product — see individual product pages for details.

Product Categories

Explore by Category

Seven category lines, each built for a different kind of food business — tap any one to see what's inside.

Miguelitos soft serve premixes deliver consistent texture, rich flavour, and reliable performance across a full range of machines. Available in classic vanilla, chocolate, and seasonal or specialty variants, our premixes are trusted by kiosks, food parks, mall concessions, and foodservice operators across the Philippines and beyond.

Bringing classic Filipino flavours to kitchens, food stalls, and dining tables — our Filipino Favorites line covers the country's most-loved traditional food and beverage products. Designed for consistent, easy preparation at any scale.

From refreshing iced teas and lemonades to milk teas and slush, our beverage line covers the full menu of today's most in-demand drinks. All formulated for consistent taste and quick preparation — ideal for food stalls, cafés, restaurants, and beverage kiosks.

A complete coffee range for businesses that take their menu seriously. Our coffee products cover everything from hot espresso and iced coffee to blended beverages and hot chocolate — available in premix formats for consistency and ease of service.

Professional-grade baking solutions for bakeries, cafés, and foodservice kitchens. Our mixes and premixes deliver reliable results batch after batch — from light chiffon cakes and fudgy brownies to fluffy pancakes, bread, mochi, and more.

Sauces, seasonings, batters, soups, and culinary mixes built for the demands of professional kitchens and food manufacturers. Our savory line helps food businesses achieve consistent, scalable flavour without compromising on quality.

Products that sit outside our core categories but are just as much a part of who we are. From Ice Scramble and Cotton Candy to novelty formats and new product concepts, our Specialty line reflects our commitment to keeping up with what's next in food and beverage.
