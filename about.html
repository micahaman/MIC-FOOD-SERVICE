About Us

About Miguelitos International Corporation

Miguelitos International Corporation is a Philippine food manufacturer and exporter specializing in food and beverage premixes, culinary solutions, and commercial foodservice equipment. From our manufacturing facility in the Philippines, we supply products to domestic and international markets across retail, foodservice, institutional, and business-to-business channels.

We manufacture under the Miguelitos brand and provide toll manufacturing services for brands that need a reliable Philippine production partner.

Our Story

Our Story

Miguelitos International Corporation has been a trusted manufacturer and franchisor since 2002.

We produce a wide range of instant food premixes and are the #1 Soft Serve Ice Cream brand in the Philippines. Our products are trusted by leading hotels, restaurants, food chains, and major supermarket partners.

Today, Miguelitos exports to the USA, Canada, UAE, Australia, Singapore, and Japan, bringing Filipino food innovations to customers around the world.

Facility

Our Facility

Our manufacturing facility is equipped for powder blending, dry premix production, filling, and packing across a range of product categories. The facility operates under strict quality assurance protocols to meet both domestic food safety standards and the requirements of our export markets.

Key areas include production and blending, a dedicated research and development unit, quality assurance, and warehousing for finished goods and raw materials.
