About Us

About Miguelitos International Corporation

Miguelitos International Corporation is a Philippine food manufacturer and exporter specializing in food and beverage premixes, culinary solutions, and commercial foodservice equipment. From our manufacturing facility in the Philippines, we supply products to domestic and international markets across retail, foodservice, institutional, and business-to-business channels.

We manufacture under the Miguelitos brand and provide toll manufacturing services for brands that need a reliable Philippine production partner.

Our Story

Our Story

From a Filipino Idea to a Growing Global Presence

Founded in 2002, Miguelitos began with a simple vision: to create high-quality food products while opening opportunities for Filipino entrepreneurs. What started with food premixes and soft serve ice cream soon grew into a manufacturing and franchising business, built through years of innovation, hard work, and a commitment to quality.

Over the years, Miguelitos expanded beyond ice cream into a diverse range of food solutions—from beverages and Filipino delicacies to bakery products, seasonings, and other specialty mixes. Alongside manufacturing, the company grew its franchise network across the Philippines and built long-standing relationships with restaurants, food businesses, supermarkets, and other industry partners.

Today, Miguelitos continues to grow beyond the Philippines, bringing its products and business concepts to international markets. As we expand, our purpose remains the same: to create quality food solutions, support the businesses we serve, and bring a familiar taste of home to Filipinos around the world.

Facility

Our Facility

Our manufacturing facility is equipped for powder blending, dry premix production, filling, and packing across a range of product categories. The facility operates under strict quality assurance protocols to meet both domestic food safety standards and the requirements of our export markets.

Key areas include production and blending, a dedicated research and development unit, quality assurance, and warehousing for finished goods and raw materials.

Compliance

Certifications & Compliance

Miguelitos International Corporation maintains the food safety and regulatory certifications required for both our domestic operations and our export markets.

PHFDA

Miguelitos is a trusted Filipino brand committed to providing quality and safe food products, with PHFDA certification as part of our commitment to food safety and quality standards.

Halal Certified

Miguelitos is Halal Certified, ensuring that our products meet established Halal standards and requirements. This certification reflects our commitment to providing quality products for our customers.

HACCP Certified

Miguelitos is HACCP Certified, following established food safety standards and practices. This certification reflects our commitment to maintaining safe and quality products.

USFDA

Miguelitos is USFDA Registered, demonstrating our commitment to maintaining quality and meeting food safety requirements for international distribution.

Current Good Manufacturing Practices

Miguelitos is compliant with Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP), reflecting our commitment to consistent quality, safety, and proper manufacturing processes.

GSO 2055-1:2015

Miguelitos is compliant with GSO 2055-1:2015, supporting our commitment to producing food products that meet recognized Halal standards. This certification helps ensure that our products are suitable for Halal markets and consumers.

UAE 2055-1:2015

Miguelitos complies with UAE 2055-1:2015, a standard for Halal food products in the United Arab Emirates. This reflects our commitment to meeting recognized Halal requirements and maintaining quality and safety in our products.
