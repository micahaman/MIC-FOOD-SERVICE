Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Miguelitos International Corporation ("Miguelitos", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through www.miguelitoscorp.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We process personal information in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Information we collect

  • Inquiry forms. When you send an inquiry through our Contact or Toll Manufacturing forms, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, company, position, email address, mobile number, country, the type of inquiry, your message, project details, and any file you choose to attach.
  • Cookies and usage data. With your consent, we use Google Tag Manager and related Google analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site (for example, pages viewed and buttons clicked). These tools set cookies and may collect your IP address, device and browser type, and approximate location.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services that deliver our fonts and scripts (Google Fonts, jsDelivr) may automatically log your IP address and browser details to serve and secure the site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and send you quotations, product information, or partnership details you asked for.
  • To route your inquiry to the right team, such as sales, export, or toll manufacturing.
  • To improve the website and our services, using analytics data only if you accept cookies.
  • To keep the site and our forms secure and to prevent spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are delivered to our sales inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-handling service. Analytics data is processed by Google. These providers process information on our behalf for the purposes above. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Cookies and your choices

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics cookies. Analytics cookies stay off unless you click Accept. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain our business records, and then delete or anonymize it. Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings.

How we protect it

The site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to inquiry emails is limited to the staff who handle them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect your information.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to erase or block, to data portability, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
