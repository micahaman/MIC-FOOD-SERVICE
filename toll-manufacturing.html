Toll Manufacturing

Your formulation. Our facility. Manufactured to your specification.

Miguelitos International Corporation offers toll manufacturing services for food and beverage brands, retailers, restaurants, and institutional buyers who need a reliable, experienced production partner in the Philippines.

We work with established brands looking to outsource production, emerging brands that need a manufacturing partner to scale, and businesses that want to develop a new product under their own label. Our facility handles formulation, blending, production, and packaging — from concept through to finished, shelf-ready product.

Capabilities

What We Can Produce

  • Powder blending and dry premix manufacturing
  • Beverage formulations — instant drinks, concentrates, and premixes
  • Bakery and dessert formulations
  • Savory mixes, seasonings, and culinary blends
  • Filling and packing across a range of packaging formats
